Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does a typical project take?

Most small business websites take 1–3 weeks from kickoff to launch, depending on scope. Online stores and larger sites can take longer. I'll give you a clear timeline before we start.

Do you only build WordPress websites?

WordPress and WooCommerce are my main focus since they're flexible and easy for you to manage afterward, but I also build lightweight custom-coded sites like this one when that's a better fit.

How much does a website cost?

It depends on the size and features you need — a landing page costs less than a full online store. Tell me about your project and I'll give you a clear quote upfront, with no surprise fees.

Do you offer support after the site is launched?

Yes. Every project includes a short period of free support after launch, and I offer ongoing maintenance if you'd like help with updates, backups or changes down the line.

Can you redesign my existing website?

Yes — whether it's an outdated WordPress site or something built elsewhere, I can rebuild it with a modern design while keeping the content and structure that already works for you.

Do I need to provide my own content and images?

Ideally yes — text and images specific to your business make the site feel authentic. If you're missing some pieces, I can help source placeholders or guide you on what to prepare.

How do we get started?

Reach out through the contact form or WhatsApp with a bit about your business and what you need. I'll follow up with questions, a quote, and a timeline before any work begins.
